# VIE — interactive study

A photo-based interactive reproduction of Tony Oursler’s **VIE (2014)**, following the artist reference supplied by the student. The initial composition uses the original artwork photograph, rather than a newly authored portrait or reconstructed video installation. Four matching image excerpts cover the two eye windows, nose, and mouth. They align with the underlying photograph until moved. Moving a feature exposes a neutral rectangular opening and adds a line to its original position. Escape restores the composition.

There is no visible navigation, text, or interface framing. SVG preserves the reference proportions on desktop and mobile. Alt descriptions and keyboard instructions remain accessible to screen readers.
